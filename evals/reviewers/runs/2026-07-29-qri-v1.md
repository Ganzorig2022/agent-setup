## Raw-output audit

Mechanical deltas are not interpreted as judgment improvements:

- Two control TypeScript “misses” explicitly reported caller-owned mutation in
  human prose. The grader missed the finding because the filename was outside
  its three-paragraph keyword window. The trailer's `file` field repairs that
  association mechanically.
- The control's two `best-effort-metadata-parse` trap flags were grader
  cross-matches; those reviews did not actually flag the documented
  best-effort parser.
- Two treatment React reviews did flag the documented mount-only analytics
  effect, while the third did not. This is genuine pair-level false-positive
  variance even though total trap count stayed unchanged. It is recorded as a
  limitation, not used to tune another prompt from one synthetic task.
- The TypeScript `helmet-security-theater` trap occurred in the same
  repetition in both arms.

The evidence supports adopting the format trailer without claiming that it
improved reviewer judgment. Post-trailer real traces, not this synthetic
matrix, determine the parse-rate gate.
